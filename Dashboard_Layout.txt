# Dashboard Layout

## Page 1: Executive Overview
- KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Profit Margin %
- Line chart: Sales by Month (use Order_Date hierarchy or Calendar table)
- Bar chart: Sales by Region
- Donut chart: Sales by Category
- Slicers: Region, Category, Order_Date

## Page 2: Product Performance
- Bar chart: Sales by Product
- Bar chart: Profit by Product
- Matrix: Category > Product with Sales, Profit, Quantity, Profit Margin %
- Scatter chart: Sales vs Profit by Product (optional)

## Page 3: Regional Analysis
- Bar chart: Sales by City
- Column chart: Profit by Region
- Matrix: Region and City with Sales, Orders, Profit

## Formatting
- Use a consistent two-colour theme, clear titles and readable labels.
- Format Sales/Profit as currency (₹ if you choose to relabel the fictional data as INR; values are generated as generic currency).
- Format percentage measures as Percentage with 1 decimal place.
- Add report title: Sales Performance Analysis | 2025.
